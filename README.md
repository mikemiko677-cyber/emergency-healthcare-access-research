## 📌 Project Overview

Access to timely emergency healthcare is an important challenge in communities where transportation, road infrastructure, distance, and the availability of medical services may affect people's ability to receive care.

This independent research project examines emergency healthcare accessibility in Ethiopia and considers how technology and improved connections between patients and healthcare providers could contribute to addressing these challenges.

The project also reflects my interest in using computer science and digital innovation to explore practical solutions to real-world problems.

## 🎯 Research Focus

The project focuses on understanding emergency healthcare access and exploring potential approaches to improving it, including:

- Identifying barriers that may prevent people from reaching emergency medical services.
- Exploring the relationship between transportation, healthcare infrastructure, and timely access to care.
- Examining how digital technologies could help people locate healthcare facilities and emergency assistance.
- Exploring opportunities for technology-supported connections between patients, healthcare facilities, and emergency transportation.
- Considering how digital health solutions could be designed around the needs of underserved communities.
