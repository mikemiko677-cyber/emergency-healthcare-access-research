# Emergency Healthcare Access in Ethiopia

## Independent Student Research Project

**Author:** Michael Yonatan Betre  
**Location:** Ethiopia  
**Year:** 2026

### About This Research

This is an independent student literature review exploring barriers to emergency healthcare access in Ethiopia.

The research focuses on:

- Emergency transportation
- Ambulance access
- Road and infrastructure challenges
- Communication
- Pre-hospital emergency care
- Waiting time
- The potential role of digital technology

I became interested in this topic after witnessing an emergency situation as a child in which a pregnant woman was being carried toward a hospital because a vehicle could not reach the area.

That experience made me ask:

> "What if there was a better way?"

I later connected this question with my interest in software development and began developing **HealthReach**, a student prototype exploring digital coordination between patients, ambulance drivers, and hospitals.

### Research Method

This project is a literature review based on published research concerning emergency medical services, ambulance utilization, and pre-hospital emergency care in Ethiopia.

No original patient survey or clinical study was conducted.

### HealthReach

HealthReach is a student prototype developed to explore how technology could support emergency coordination.

The prototype is not a clinically validated medical device and has not been tested in real emergency situations.
